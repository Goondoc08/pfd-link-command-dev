Tour Tasks — every day

  • Blow bays out
  • Keep dorm rooms neat and presentable with beds made
  • Clean kitchen, wash and put away dishes
  • Start laundry and fold towels
  • Take out all trash, inside and bays
  • Vacuum door mats and entry ways
  • Sweep and mop floors
All staff split the assigned tasks daily as instructed by the Officer. It is the responsibility of the crews to establish an action plan to ensure all tasks are completed.
All apparatus in each station should be checked on the first day of each tour to ensure readiness, and documented appropriately.
