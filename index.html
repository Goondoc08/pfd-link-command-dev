Tips & Known Limits

Helpful tips

Pulsara looks zoomed in / cut off
The web version renders large on a phone. Pinch to zoom out, or in Chrome tap ⋮ → Settings → Accessibility and lower Text scaling; on iPhone tap ᴀA in the address bar and pick a smaller zoom. The setting sticks for that site.
Install it to your home screen
Runs full screen with no address bar, and works with no signal. See Add to home screen at the bottom of the page.
Star what you use
Tap the ☆ on any tile to pin it to a Favorites row at the top. Saved on your phone only — everyone's is their own.
Set your mando group once
Open Mando Schedule and pick your group. After that your next dates show on the tile, and your days highlight amber in the calendar.
Tap the shift bar
The date bar at the top opens a month calendar — shift letter per day, plus the mando group scheduled that day.
It works with no signal
Once you've opened it, the flow chart, station duties, mando schedule and district map all stay available offline.

Known limits

Search only covers tile names
It matches link names and descriptions, not the text inside pages. Searching "sock hood" won't jump into Station Duties. No typo tolerance either — "hantevy" finds nothing.
Web Only tiles won't open the app
Pulsara opens the website even if you have the app installed. No fix for this right now.
Store tiles land on the store page
Mindbase and EMS Protocols open the App Store / Play Store listing. Tap Open there to launch the app. No fix for this right now.
EMS Protocols on iPhone
iPhone has no App Store listing for the Pearland build — it's handed out by promo code, so the tile goes to AcidRemap's download page.
Off City WiFi on HR Suite
HR Suite won't load on the station network. Use cell data.
